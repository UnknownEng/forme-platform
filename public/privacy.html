Privacy, in plain words

What we keep. What we don't.

Last updated 16 November 2026. This is a summary for shoppers and merchants using Forme while it is in pilot. It will be replaced by a full policy reviewed under Pakistan's data protection law before general launch.

Shoppers

  • What we store: your height, weight, optional body measurements (chest, waist, hips, inseam), whether you buy women's or men's sizes, and how you like clothes to fit.
  • What we never ask for: your name, phone number, address, email, CNIC or photos.
  • Where it lives: a random profile code is saved in your browser, and the measurements are saved on Forme's servers so the store can show your size.
  • What it is used for: only to recommend sizes and to improve size accuracy (for example, learning that a product runs small). We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • Delete it any time: tap Forget me in the size panel. This removes your profile from our servers and your browser.

Merchants

  • We store your account details (company name, work email, a hashed password), your products and size charts, and anonymous shopper events (widget opened, size recommended, added to cart, purchase value, keep/return outcome).
  • Secret API keys and passwords are stored only as one-way hashes.
  • You can ask us to delete your account and all its data.

For developers

A shopper profile can be erased with DELETE /v1/profiles/me. See the API docs.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: reach the Forme team through the contact details on the pilot agreement you received.

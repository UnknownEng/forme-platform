The problem

"Medium" means something different
at every single brand.

30–40%

of online apparel orders are commonly reported as returned worldwide, far more than any other category.

#1

reason shoppers give for sending clothes back: the size or fit was wrong.

COD

Most Pakistani online orders are cash on delivery. A size doubt at the door becomes a refused parcel and a wasted two-way courier trip.

Return figures are widely cited global industry estimates; Pakistani figures vary by brand and courier and are rarely published. Plug in your own numbers below.

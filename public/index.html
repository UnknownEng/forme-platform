How it works

From size chart to confident checkout
in three steps.

01
⇄

Sync your size charts

Push products through the REST API, bulk-import JSON, or edit charts in the console. Tops, bottoms and dresses, any size labels.

PUT /v1/products/{sku}
02
◎

Shoppers answer three questions

Height, weight and how they like clothes to fit. Exact measurements are optional. The profile is saved once and reused on every product.

<div data-forme-sku="…">
03
↻

Recommend, then learn

The engine returns a size, a confidence score and fit notes. Keep/return feedback recalibrates every product that runs small or large.

POST /v1/feedback
